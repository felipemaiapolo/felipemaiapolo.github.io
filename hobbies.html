⛵ Yachtness

How yacht rock is your song?

A playful machine-learning tool that scores any song from 0 to 100 on how much it sounds like yacht rock — the smooth, polished soft rock of roughly 1976–1984. Search a song to get its Yachtness score, see its percentile among nearly 2,000 rated songs, and find out which classic yacht rock artists it most resembles.
